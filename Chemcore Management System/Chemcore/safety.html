Safety Guidelines

Standard operating precautions

  • Always verify container labels and Safety Data Sheets (SDS) before handling any chemical.
  • Store acids and bases in separate, clearly marked cabinets to avoid reactive mixing.
  • Ensure fume hoods and ventilation systems are active before opening volatile solvents.
  • Never return unused chemical to its original container — dispose per site protocol.
  • Keep spill kits and eyewash stations accessible within 10 seconds of any work area.
  • Report any damaged container, leak, or unusual odor to the Safety Officer immediately.

Emergency Procedure

Follow these steps in order during any chemical incident

1

Contain & Alert

Evacuate the immediate area and pull the nearest alarm. Alert coworkers verbally if possible.

2

Notify Response Team

Call the Emergency Response hotline and provide chemical name, location, and severity.

3

Administer First Aid

Use eyewash/shower stations for exposure. Do not move injured personnel unless necessary.

4

Report & Review

File an incident report below within 24 hours. Safety team will review and follow up.
